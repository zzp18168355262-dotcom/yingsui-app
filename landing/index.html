Why Shadowing English

Less passive watching. More English you can actually say.

A good shadowing English routine needs precise timing and fewer distractions. This app is designed around one job: helping you hear a line, understand it, and say it back.

01 — YOUR MATERIALS

Learn from videos you care about

Import your own local video and subtitle files. Your study library stays yours; no bundled third-party course catalog is required.

02 — AI, IN CONTEXT

Get subtitles ready for practice

Generate AI subtitles with word-level timing where supported, then use them for more accurate, natural line-by-line practice.

03 — REPEAT WITH INTENTION

Stay with the sentence

Pause, replay, follow the subtitle, and switch on shadowing mode—without turning your practice into a generic flashcard drill.

FAQ

Questions about shadowing English and the app.

What is shadowing English?

Shadowing is a speaking practice method: listen to natural English and repeat immediately, matching pronunciation, rhythm, and connected speech.

Is there a web version?

Not yet. Shadowing English is currently a downloadable app for Android, Windows, macOS, and Linux.

Can I use my own English videos?

Yes. Import your own local video and subtitle materials. The project intentionally does not ship third-party video packs or commercial subtitle libraries.

How does AI help with shadowing?

AI can generate timed subtitles for supported providers. The app is also continuing to improve sentence-level explanations and shadowing guidance.
